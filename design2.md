# Design System 2 Specification — SKORA Healthcare & Doctor Specialisation Portal

Derived from the reference mockup image and populated with real clinical growth content from `https://skorasoft.com/specialisation`, this design system specifies a pristine, high-end modern medical aesthetic with soft cyan/blue accents (`#0DB8B5` / `#0A66C2`), clean rounded card containers (`rounded-[2.5rem]`), and elegant typography.

---

## 🎨 Color Palette & Aesthetic Tokens

### 1. Canvas & Structural Tokens
- **Background Canvas**: Soft Light Sky Slate (`#F0F7FF` / `#F8FAFC`)
- **Hero & Card Containers**: Pristine White (`#FFFFFF`) & Soft Glassmorphic Overlays (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.85)`) with rounded corners (`rounded-[2.5rem]`)
- **Primary Teal/Cyan Accent**: `#0DB8B5` / `#0CB8B6`
- **Deep Slate Typography**: `#0F172A` (Slate-900)
- **Sub-Text Typography**: `#475569` (Slate-600)
- **Gold Rating Accent**: `#F59E0B` (Amber-500)
