# Design System 1 Specification — Consultiv Modern Business Theme

Derived from the Dribbble reference design ("Consultiv - Modern business consulting website"), this design system establishes a high-impact, modern, clean, and vibrant visual aesthetic tailored for SkoraSoft Digital Pvt. Ltd. and enterprise client pages.

---

## 🎨 Color Palette & Aesthetic Tokens

### 1. Base Canvases
- **Top Hero Canvas (Light Warm Linen / Crisp Porcelain)**: `#F8F9F6` / `#FFFFFF`
- **Text Primary**: `#0B1310` (Midnight Slate Black)
- **Text Secondary**: `#5A6E65` (Muted Sage Grey)
- **Accent Leaf Green**: `#22C55E` / `#16A34A` / `#3BB75E`
- **Dark Section Background**: `#081710` / `#0A1C14` / `#07130E`
- **Dark Card Glass**: `rgba(18, 36, 29, 0.85)` / `#12241C` with `border: 1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`
- **Featured Green Card**: `linear-gradient(135deg, #1E824C 0%, #27AE60 100%)`

### 2. Gradient Wave Transition
- **Top-to-Dark Liquid Wave**:
  - Smooth curved SVG / CSS gradient transition blending from `#F8F9F6` into deep `#081710` emerald dark space.

### 3. Typography Hierarchy
- **Headline Display**: `Plus Jakarta Sans` / `Outfit`, ultra-bold (`font-black`), tracking tight.
- **Body & Subtext**: `Inter` / `Plus Jakarta Sans`, font-medium.
- **Metric Highlight**: Large numeric callouts (`200k+`, `250+`, `99.4%`).

---

## 🏛️ Layout Architecture

### 1. Header & Social Proof Eyebrow
- Centered social proof capsule: `( Guest favorite ) [avatars] 200k+ Total user`
- Clean brand header with logo and navigation links (`Home`, `Services`, `Healthcare Portal`, `News & Blogs`, `Contact`).

### 2. Hero Display Section
- **Headline**:
  `DRIVING BUSINESS`
  `GROWTH & SUCCESS` (where `GROWTH & SUCCESS` is rendered in vibrant leaf green `#22C55E`).
- **Subheadline**: *"Smart digital engineering & growth marketing that actually helps your business scale with high-converting websites, AI SEO, and solid tech strategies."*
- **Primary CTA Button**: Sleek black pill button `"Request a Strategy Session"`.
- **5-Item Rounded Thumbnail Gallery**: A horizontal row of 5 rounded images (flower macro, professional portrait, holding camera, minimalist green chair design, gallery walk) with subtle hover scale effects.

### 3. Metrics & Brand Overview Section
- **Display Subheader**: `WE'VE GROWN INTO A GO-TO PARTNER FOR BUSINESS GROWTH AND SMART DIGITAL EXCELLENCE.`
- **Content Columns**: Two narrative paragraphs explaining SkoraSoft's approach to web development, performance marketing, and client success.
- **Stat Counters**:
  - `200k+` — Clients served & verified leads generated
  - `250+` — Enterprise digital products & apps delivered
  - `99.4%` — Client retention rate
- **Featured Visual**: Rounded image card showing creative strategy in action.

### 4. Dark Wave Transition & "WHAT WE'RE UP TO" Section
- Dynamic curved green gradient wave transitioning into `#081710`.
- Dark card grid featuring SkoraSoft core offerings:
  - Website Design & Engineering
  - Digital Marketing & Local SEO
  - Mobile Apps & SaaS Development
  - Cloud Infrastructure & CRM Automation

### 5. Horizontal Value Proposition Cards
- `DEDICATED SUPPORT`: "From initial consultation to final deployment, we stand by you with clarity and 24/7 guidance."
- `PROVEN EXPERTISE`: "Years of full-stack engineering and marketing mastery helping brands achieve lasting outcomes."
- `DATA-DRIVEN INSIGHTS`: "Every decision backed by analytics, user testing, and performance metrics."
- `CUSTOMIZED SOLUTIONS`: "Zero templates. 100% custom-crafted digital products tailored to your goals."

### 6. Dark Pricing Matrix ("PRICING THAT WORKS FOR YOU")
- 4-column pricing layout:
  1. Free Strategy Audit (`₹0/mo`)
  2. Starter Web & SEO (`₹15,000/mo`)
  3. Growth Marketing (`₹25,000/mo`)
  4. Featured Emerald Enterprise Suite (`₹49,000/mo`) — Highlighted with vibrant green background and white text.

### 7. Liquid Emerald CTA Banner ("READY TO TRANSFORM YOUR BUSINESS?")
- Wavy liquid emerald glow background (`background: radial-gradient(circle at center, #1E824C 0%, #081710 80%)`).
- Floating rotated photo cards on left and right sides.
- Center action pill button: `"Get Started"`.

### 8. Testimonials ("PEOPLE REALLY DIG IT")
- Clean review cards with doctor, startup founder, and enterprise client feedback.
